Virtual Care, Real Continuity

Telehealth at KampieDa

KampieDa offers secure, physician-led telehealth visits for patients in Virginia, Maryland, Washington D.C., and New York. Telehealth lets you connect with your care team without the trip to our office — while maintaining the same clinical standards, continuity, and privacy as an in-person visit.

Call (540) 446-2610 or email info@kampiedaMedicalGroup.com to schedule a telehealth appointment.

Physician-Led Telehealth Care

All telehealth visits at KampieDa are conducted by licensed providers who are authorized to practice medicine in your state. Your virtual visit is a true medical appointment, guided by the same clinical judgment and evidence-based care you’d expect in our office.

During your telehealth visit, your provider may:

  • Review your medical history and current concerns
  • Evaluate symptoms and discuss possible diagnoses
  • Prescribe medications when appropriate
  • Order lab work or imaging
  • Coordinate follow-up care and specialist referrals
  • Maintain detailed medical records as part of your chart

This ensures continuity, safety, and accountability across every visit — virtual or in person.

What Telehealth Is Best Suited For

  • Follow-up visits and ongoing care
  • Medication management and prescription refills
  • Anxiety, stress, and mental health check-ins
  • Review of lab and imaging results
  • Chronic condition monitoring, such as hypertension
  • Preventive care discussions and health planning

When an In-Person Visit Is Recommended

  • New or worsening symptoms
  • Physical exams
  • Blood tests, imaging, or in-office procedures
  • Vaccinations
  • Conditions that require direct examination

Your provider will help determine the right setting so care stays safe and effective, without unnecessary delays.

What to Expect During a Telehealth Visit

Telehealth visits are private, confidential, and focused on your health concerns. During your appointment, you’ll meet with your provider by secure video or phone. Your provider will:

  • Ask detailed questions about your symptoms and history
  • Discuss possible diagnoses and treatment options
  • Prescribe medication when appropriate
  • Recommend testing or an in-person evaluation if needed

Technology & Privacy

Telehealth visits typically require:

  • A smartphone, tablet, or computer
  • A stable internet or phone connection
  • A private, quiet space

No specialized technical knowledge is required — our staff can walk you through the setup beforehand and assist with any issues. All visits are conducted using secure platforms that meet medical privacy and confidentiality standards.

Insurance & Telehealth Visits

KampieDa accepts most major insurance plans, including Medicaid and Medicare, for telehealth visits. Coverage can depend on your specific plan and the type of appointment — our administrative team can help verify your coverage and answer billing questions before your visit.

Frequently Asked Questions About Telehealth

Is telehealth confidential and secure?

Yes. Telehealth visits follow the same privacy standards as in-person medical care.

Can prescriptions be provided during telehealth visits?

In many cases, yes — your provider will determine what’s appropriate based on your visit and applicable regulations.

Can new patients use telehealth?

Yes, new patients are welcome. Some initial visits may require an in-person component depending on your care needs.

What happens if labs or imaging are needed?

Your provider will order any needed labs or imaging and coordinate scheduling at our office or a nearby facility.

Can telehealth replace in-person primary care?

No. Telehealth is not a replacement for the patient-provider relationship — it’s an extension of it. Virtual visits are fully integrated with your ongoing medical care and record.
