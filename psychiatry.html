Psychiatry

Psychiatric Care at KampieDa

Our psychiatric team is committed to providing compassionate, comprehensive mental health care to adolescents and adults — in person and by telehealth, across Virginia, Maryland, Washington D.C., and New York.

Our Mission

At KampieDa, our mission is to help patients lead fulfilling lives by offering personalized, evidence-based psychiatric treatment in a warm and supportive environment. We believe in whole-person care — addressing the unique needs and concerns of each patient to support lasting mental wellness.

Our Approach

We understand that seeking psychiatric care can be a significant step toward healing and growth. That’s why we prioritize creating a safe, judgment-free space where patients feel heard and respected.

Our psychiatric providers use a collaborative approach, working closely with patients to develop tailored treatment plans that may integrate therapy, medication management, and lifestyle modifications as needed. We involve patients in every treatment decision, because feeling comfortable, confident, and reassured is central to lasting care.

Services Offered

  • Psychiatric evaluation, diagnosis & treatment
  • Medication management
  • Telepsychiatry services
  • ADHD evaluation & support
  • Individual supportive psychotherapy
  • Couples therapy
  • Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)
  • Motivational Interviewing
  • Mindfulness-based interventions

Our Providers

Our psychiatric team brings a wealth of expertise and compassion to every visit. We take the time to truly get to know our patients, and we coordinate care with any outside providers — including therapists and other physicians — per your request and consent. With diverse backgrounds and specialties, our team is dedicated to delivering the highest quality of care. You’re in expert hands.
