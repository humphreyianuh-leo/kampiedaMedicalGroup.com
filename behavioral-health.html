Your Care Team

Support That Surrounds You

You’re not expected to figure this out alone. Throughout your care, you’re guided by a coordinated team of professionals who work together to support your progress and healing.

Therapists

Licensed clinicians who lead individual and family sessions — here to listen, guide, and help build a strong foundation for healing.

Psychiatric Team

Psychiatric providers with a family-centered approach to medication management and clinical wellness.

Group Facilitators

Experienced facilitators who make group spaces feel safe and welcoming, so conversations stay relevant and meaningful.

Our Methods

Specialized in Multiple Approaches

You’ll build the strategies and skills you need for lasting stability — our clinicians draw from multiple methods and adapt them to you.

DBT

Dialectical Behavior Therapy

Teaches mindfulness-based and action-oriented coping skills from the start.

CBT

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy

Builds insight and coping skills through structured, logic-based practice.

CFT

Compassion-Focused Therapy

Cultivates self-compassion to help reduce internalized shame.

MI

Motivational Interviewing

Builds motivation for change through supportive, structured conversation.

TF-CBT

Trauma-Focused CBT

Helps process and reframe difficult experiences to reduce distress.

FT

Family Therapy

Strengthens communication and connection within the family system.
