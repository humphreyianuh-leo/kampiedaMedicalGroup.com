Weight Loss

Weight Loss Medications

KampieDa offers physician-guided weight loss medication management as part of our primary care services. Your provider will review your health history, current medications, and goals to determine whether medication treatment is appropriate for you — and which option may be the best fit.

Who Qualifies

Are You a Candidate?

Clinical guidelines generally support medication treatment for adults who have attempted lifestyle changes and meet either BMI threshold below. Your provider will personalize a treatment plan based on your full health history, comorbidities, and goals.

BMI ≥ 30

Obesity — without a weight-related condition required.

BMI ≥ 27

Overweight with at least one comorbidity (hypertension, diabetes, sleep apnea, high cholesterol, etc.).

This is general clinical guidance, not a guarantee of eligibility or treatment outcome. A full evaluation with your provider determines what’s right for you.

Coverage & Cost

Insurance & Pricing

Coverage for anti-obesity medications varies by insurance plan. We help you navigate prior authorization, verify your benefits, and find the most affordable path — including manufacturer savings programs where available.

Coverage Verification

We check your plan before prescribing and identify which medications are covered.

Prior Authorization

Most GLP-1s require prior authorization — we handle the paperwork and advocate on your behalf.

Cash-Pay Options

Self-pay pricing is available for patients without coverage — ask our team for current rates.

Find the Right Medication for You

Every patient is different. Your provider will review your health history, goals, and insurance to recommend the best treatment for you — injectable, oral, or none at all.
