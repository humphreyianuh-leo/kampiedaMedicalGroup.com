Whole-Person Care for Mind & Body

Your health deserves care that considers the whole person. If you are well, we can help you stay that way. If you are sick, we can help you get better. Our medical and behavioral health providers work together to support better physical and emotional wellness.

Most of our providers are Family Physicians, Psychiatric Providers, or Licensed Counselors. They provide care to people of all ages — children, adults, and families — regardless of your ability to pay. No matter who you are, we’re here to help.

We offer:

  • Primary care check-ups & preventive medicine
  • Psychiatric evaluation & medication management
  • Individual, family & behavioral health counseling
  • Secure telehealth visits for medical & behavioral care
  • Care for chronic conditions, including diabetes & hypertension
  • …and much more
